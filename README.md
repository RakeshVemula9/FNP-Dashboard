# 🎁 FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard built to analyze FNP sales performance across occasions, product categories, order time, delivery time, months, products and cities.

The project brings the supporting data and final interactive dashboard together to provide a clear view of revenue, orders, customer spending and delivery performance.

## 📖 About the Project

FNP (Ferns N Petals) is a gifting platform offering products across different occasions such as anniversaries, birthdays, Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan and Valentine's Day.

I wanted to analyze the sales data to understand which occasions, product categories, products and cities contribute the most to overall revenue.

I built this project entirely in Microsoft Excel.

The workbook contains the supporting data, analysis and final interactive dashboard.

The dashboard is designed to provide a quick overview of overall business performance while allowing the analysis to be explored using interactive filters.

# 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

## 🎉 Revenue by Occasion

The dashboard compares revenue generated across different occasions.

The occasions included in the analysis are:

* Anniversary
* Birthday
* Diwali
* Holi
* Raksha Bandhan
* Valentine's Day
* All Occasions

### Key Observation

Among the individual occasions shown in the dashboard, **Anniversary** generates the highest revenue, followed by **Raksha Bandhan**.

This indicates that occasion-based gifting plays an important role in the overall revenue generated by the business.

---

## 🛍️ Revenue by Category

The category analysis compares revenue across major product categories.

The categories shown include:

* Cake
* Colors
* Mugs
* Plants
* Raksha Bandhan
* Soft Toys
* Sweets

### Key Observation

**Colors** is the highest-revenue category in the dashboard.

Soft Toys and Sweets also contribute substantial revenue compared with the other categories.

---

## 🕐 Revenue by Hour

The hourly analysis shows how revenue changes according to the order time throughout the day.

The dashboard tracks revenue across the 24-hour period.

### Key Observation

Revenue varies considerably throughout the day, with several stronger periods visible during the afternoon and evening.

This type of analysis can help identify the periods when order activity and revenue generation are strongest.

---

## 📅 Revenue by Month

The monthly analysis compares revenue across the months of the year.

### Key Observation

Revenue is not evenly distributed throughout the year.

The dashboard shows notable peaks during **February** and **August**, while several other months record considerably lower revenue.

These peaks may be associated with seasonal or occasion-driven gifting demand.

# ⚙️ Project Workflow

## 1. Understanding the Data

I first reviewed the available customer, order and product data to understand the structure of the dataset and identify the fields required for analysis.

This helped determine which metrics and dimensions would be useful for the dashboard.

## 2. Preparing the Data

The available data was organized and checked so that the relevant order, product, customer and date fields could be used consistently for analysis.

## 3. Creating Supporting Analysis

Supporting Pivot Tables were created to analyze:

* Revenue by occasion
* Revenue by category
* Revenue by hour
* Revenue by month
* Top products by revenue
* Orders by city

## 4. Building the KPIs

The dashboard includes KPI cards for:

* Total Orders
* Total Revenue
* Order-Delivery Time
* Average Customer Spend

These KPIs provide a quick summary of overall performance.

## 5. Creating the Visualizations

Different chart types were used according to the type of analysis being presented.

For example:

* Column charts for category and occasion comparisons
* Line charts for time-based trends
* Bar charts for city and product comparisons
* KPI cards for headline business metrics

## 6. Adding Interactivity

Interactive filters were added for:

* Order Date
* Delivery Date
* Occasion

These filters allow users to explore specific periods and occasions.

## 7. Designing the Dashboard

Once the analysis was complete, I focused on the presentation and usability of the dashboard.

I worked on:

* Layout
* Alignment
* Spacing
* Number formatting
* KPI placement
* Chart sizes
* Borders
* Slicer placement
* Visual hierarchy
* Colour consistency

The final dashboard uses a green and white visual theme.

# ▶️ How to Use the Dashboard

1. Download `FNP_Sales_Dashboard.xlsx` from this repository.
2. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
3. Go to the dashboard worksheet.
4. Use the Order Date timeline to filter the required period.
5. Use the Delivery Date timeline to filter by delivery period.
6. Use the Occasion slicer to select a specific occasion.
7. Review how the connected KPIs and visualizations change based on the selected filters.
8. Clear the filters to return to the complete dataset.

> **Note:** For the best experience and full slicer/timeline functionality, open the workbook using the desktop version of Microsoft Excel.
